About This App

This is installed as an app on your home screen, so it opens even with no signal — new transactions always save instantly on this device first. When you're back online, pending transactions sync to the "Transactions" tab of the CGS Google Sheet automatically, or tap "Sync Pending" from the History tab.

Reset

This device's saved transactions live only in this browser until they sync. Clearing data removes anything not yet synced — make sure "Sync Pending" shows 0 first.
